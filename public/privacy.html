CasaPrice Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

CasaPrice shows estimated property prices per square meter for cities and districts in Romania. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. CasaPrice has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

The addresses you search for, the estimates you save, and your settings (such as the EUR to RON rate) are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them. You can delete them at any time with "Clear history" and "Clear saved" in Settings, or by deleting the app.

Third-party services

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google may receive your device's IP address and standard request information, as with any web font. See Google's privacy policy for details. The app does not send your searches or saved estimates to any server.

About the prices

Estimates are based on published average asking prices and are directional only. They are not valuations, financial advice, or offers.

Children

CasaPrice does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
